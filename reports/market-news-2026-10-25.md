### Situational Awareness LP — Special Section

**Background:** Situational Awareness LP is the hedge fund founded by Leopold Aschenbrenner, the former OpenAI researcher known for his influential AI forecasting essay "Situational Awareness."

**The July 2026 Collapse:**
- The fund suffered approximately **$35 billion in losses** in July 2026 — one of the largest single trading losses in history
- Assets fell from ~$45B to ~$10B after leveraged AI infrastructure bets (including SK Hynix, semiconductor names) moved sharply against it
- The fund reportedly used **leverage of up to 400%**, amplifying losses when AI infrastructure stocks declined
- Short positions in software companies like Adobe also moved against the fund
- Citadel purchased most of the fund's public stock portfolio in a forced liquidation
- The fund was forced to **sell all publicly traded stock positions**

**Current Status (as of latest reports):**
- The fund retained its **private holdings**, most notably a ~$5B stake in Anthropic
- In early August, the fund returned to investing with a **$400M allocation**, approximately one week after the collapse
- **Q2 2026 13F** (filed August 14, reflecting June 30 holdings — before the collapse): showed $20.2B across 26–46 holdings. Largest position was SanDisk (SNDK) at ~$5.67B, followed by Micron
- **Q1 2026 13F**: showed 42 holdings valued at $13.7B, including put options on semiconductor and AI names
- No Q3 2026 filing is available yet (deadline mid-November). Given the forced liquidation, the Q3 filing is expected to show minimal or zero public equity holdings

**Key takeaway:** Situational Awareness LP's story is a cautionary tale about extreme leverage in concentrated AI trades. The fund's Q3 13F (when filed) will likely confirm the full exit from public markets reported in July.
