1. **Alpenglow Upgrade Goes Live — Biggest Solana Protocol Overhaul Since 2020**
   - Solana activated the Alpenglow consensus upgrade on mainnet today (around 14:00–14:50 UTC), replacing the TowerBFT voting system that has been in place since the March 2020 mainnet launch. This is described as the most significant change to Solana's core protocol in over six years.
   - Source: [CoinDesk](https://coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/21/solana-s-alpenglow-its-biggest-network-overhaul-since-2020-goes-live-wednesday)

2. **Transaction Finality Slashed to 150 Milliseconds**
   - With Alpenglow active, Solana's transaction finality drops from ~12.8 seconds to approximately 150 milliseconds. Votes now move off-chain, while the SVM (execution layer) and fee structure remain unchanged.
   - Source: [KuCoin News](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/solana-activates-alpenglow-upgrade-reducing-transaction-finality-to-150ms)

3. **Validator Costs Cut ~60%; Flat Fee of 0.8 SOL per Epoch**
   - Under Alpenglow, validators pay a flat fee of 0.8 SOL per epoch (burned), replacing per-vote fees. CoinDesk estimates this reduces validator costs by approximately 60%, potentially improving network economics and participation.
   - Source: [P2P.org Analysis](https://p2p.org/economy/alpenglow-solana-staking-for-institutions/)

4. **Anza CEO: "Most Ambitious Blockchain Upgrade Since Ethereum's Merge"**
   - Anza's CEO publicly called Alpenglow the most ambitious blockchain protocol upgrade since Ethereum's transition to Proof of Stake. Critics, however, warn that flat validator fees could favor well-funded operators and increase centralization risks.
   - Source: [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/solanas-alpenglow-arrives-oct-21-major-speed-boost/)

5. **LI.FI Adds Solana Route for Ondo Finance Tokenized US Stocks/ETFs**
   - Cross-chain protocol LI.FI added a Solana route for Ondo Finance on October 19, enabling access to tokenized US stocks and ETFs directly on the Solana network. This expands Solana's real-world asset (RWA) ecosystem ahead of Alpenglow's launch.
