- **Key takeaway:** Leadership vacuum at the world's most important AI chipmaker creates uncertainty across the entire AI supply chain.
- *Sources: CNN, PBS, Nvidia Press Release*

**2. Jensen Huang Reportedly Considering Return to NVIDIA**
Forbes reports Huang has spoken to allies about returning. Multiple billionaires including Elon Musk signed an open letter calling for his reinstatement. FT reports Nvidia's board has also approached Arm CEO Rene Haas as a potential permanent CEO.
