### Top News

1. **Alpenglow Mainnet Upgrade Confirmed for October 21**
   Solana has confirmed that the Alpenglow consensus upgrade will activate on mainnet on **October 21, 2026 at ~14:50–15:00 UTC** (epoch 1064). The upgrade replaces TowerBFT — Solana's consensus mechanism since 2020 — targeting finality of ~150ms, down from the current ~12.8 seconds (~99% improvement).
   *Source:* [KuCoin Flash](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/solana-confirms-alpenglow-upgrade-on-october-21) | [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/solana-sets-october-21-for-massive-alpenglow-upgrade-that-could-make-transactions-99-faster-to-finalize/)

2. **Voting Moves Off-Chain — On-Chain TPS Metrics Will Drop**
   With Alpenglow, validators will exchange consensus votes directly rather than recording them on-chain. This means reported on-chain TPS and transaction count metrics will decrease even though real economic activity stays the same. Data platforms are expected to update their metrics methodology.
   *Source:* [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/solanas-alpenglow-arrives-oct-21-major-speed-boost/) | [Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/10/16/solana-network-upgrade-alpenglow/)

3. **SIMD-0326 Passed with 98.27% Validator Support**
   The governance proposal for Alpenglow (SIMD-0326) received 98.27% approval among validator votes cast — one of the highest for a major Solana upgrade. Validators require new software and a 0.8 SOL per-epoch fee associated with vote accounts under the new system.
   *Source:* [Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2026/10/16/solana-network-upgrade-alpenglow/)

4. **Price Analysis: $80–$95 Support Zone Holds Ahead of Upgrade**
   Technical analysts note Solana consolidating above the $80–$95 support range. With Alpenglow 4 days away, attention is on whether a "sell the news" reaction or a bullish catalyst plays out post-activation.
   *Source:* [Blockonomi](https://blockonomi.com/solana-price-analysis-80-95-support-holds-as-alpenglow-upgrade-nears/)

5. **Ledger App and Validator Tooling Updates for Alpenglow**
   Ledger released updated apps relevant to Solana validator operations ahead of the Oct 21 activation. Validator operators have been advised to upgrade their software stack before the cutover.
   *Source:* [CoinGabbar](https://www.coingabbar.com/en/solana-news-today-ledger-atom-seeker-sol-price)
