### Situational Awareness LP (Leopold Aschenbrenner)

**MAJOR NEWS: Fund Collapsed from $45B to ~$10B — Forced Liquidation**

Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness LP, the high-profile AI-focused hedge fund, suffered a catastrophic collapse in late July 2026. The fund went from managing ~$45 billion to being forced to unwind all public stock positions after a historic momentum reversal.

**What happened:**
- The fund was leveraged up to 400%, concentrated in AI infrastructure longs (SK Hynix, CoreWeave, Bloom Energy, SanDisk) while shorting software companies (Adobe and others) it viewed as vulnerable to AI disruption
- A sharp momentum reversal hit both sides: AI infrastructure stocks declined while shorted software names rallied sharply
- Margin calls triggered compulsory sales; the fund was forced to sell its entire leveraged public stock portfolio to Citadel at a discount
- The $8.46B in AI-chip put options (against NVIDIA, Broadcom, AMD, Oracle, VanEck Semiconductor ETF) were also unwound

**Current status:** The fund has unwound all leveraged public positions. It remains unclear whether the fund will continue operations.
