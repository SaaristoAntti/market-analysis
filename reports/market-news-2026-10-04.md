#### The Collapse (July 2026)
- Four AI infrastructure stocks fell **>35%** in a single month
- Fund leverage was reportedly running at **4x**
- Prime brokers at Bank of America, Goldman Sachs, and JPMorgan began marketing holdings
- The fund was forced to **sell its entire public stock portfolio** to Citadel in one massive block trade
- The unwind contributed to broader AI sector volatility

#### Current Status

- According to Bloomberg, Aschenbrenner's team sought new capital after the liquidation of public positions
- CNBC reported the unwind was "only one reason the AI trade is back" — suggesting the forced selling created buying opportunities

**Key takeaway:** The Situational Awareness LP blowup is a cautionary tale about concentrated leverage in a single theme, even when the thesis is directionally correct. The fund's Q2 holdings (Micron, SanDisk, TSMC, Bloom Energy) reflected a bet on the physical infrastructure of AI — a thesis that has since recovered, making the forced liquidation particularly painful.
