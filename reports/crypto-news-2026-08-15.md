**1. Controversial EIP-8363 Would Progressively Burn Staking Rewards**
Six prominent Ethereum researchers published a new proposal (EIP-8363 / EIP-8361) that would gradually burn an increasing share of validator rewards as total staked ETH rises, reaching a full burn at approximately 60.25M ETH staked (roughly half the supply). The aim is to cap staking participation by making additional stake less profitable, addressing concerns that surging staking yields drive ETH into large centralised providers and undermine decentralisation. Aave founder Stani Kulechov emerged as one of the loudest critics, arguing the proposal makes Ethereum staking yields unpredictable and will weaken institutional demand.
