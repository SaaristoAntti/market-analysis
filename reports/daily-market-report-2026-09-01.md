**The biggest hedge fund story of the summer.** Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness LP — the AI-focused hedge fund backed by the Collison brothers, Daniel Gross, and Nat Friedman — suffered a catastrophic collapse in July 2026.

**What happened:**
- The fund peaked at ~$45 billion in AUM, making it one of the fastest-growing hedge funds in history
- In July 2026, the fund lost ~$35 billion due to leverage reportedly running as high as 4x
- Losses came from long positions in AI infrastructure stocks (SK Hynix, CoreWeave) declining sharply while short positions in software companies (Adobe) moved against the fund
- Aschenbrenner was forced to sell a majority of public equity holdings to Ken Griffin's Citadel at a discount
