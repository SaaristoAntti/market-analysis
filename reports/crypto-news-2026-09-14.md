### Situational Awareness LP (Leopold Aschenbrenner) -- Major Collapse & SEC Investigation

**Background:** Situational Awareness LP is the hedge fund founded by Leopold Aschenbrenner, former OpenAI researcher, launched in July 2024. The fund posted returns exceeding 1,000% from launch through June 2026 and peaked at ~$45 billion in assets under management.

**Q2 2026 13F Holdings (as of June 30, 2026):**
- **Total portfolio value:** $20.2 billion across 26 positions
- **Top holdings:**
  - SanDisk (SNDK): $5.7B (largest position)
  - Micron Technology (MU): $5.6B
  - Bloom Energy (BE): Major long position + call options
  - Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM): Significant holding
  - Nebius Group (YNDX): Notable position
- **Options book:** Dramatically reduced put exposure from $8.46B in Q1 to just $5M in Q2. Opened new calls in SNDK, MU, and TSM.

**The July Collapse:**
- Fund used up to **400% leverage** on concentrated semiconductor/memory bets
- SanDisk fell 47% in July, Micron dropped 29%, Bloom Energy -32%, TSM -15%, Nebius -31%
- **Margin calls** from prime brokers (Bank of America, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase) forced a distressed sale
- **Citadel (Ken Griffin) acquired** the publicly traded holdings in a distressed deal
- Fund went from ~$45B to ~$10B in assets -- a loss of roughly **$35 billion**
- **SEC has subpoenaed banks** involved in the fund's prime brokerage relationships

**Post-Collapse Activity:**
- The fund is reportedly buying options again despite the massive drawdown
- SEC investigation is ongoing, focusing on the extreme leverage and prime brokerage arrangements

**Key Takeaway:** The Situational Awareness collapse is the largest single-fund blow-up since Archegos in 2021, driven by extreme leverage on concentrated semiconductor bets. The SEC investigation and the fund's attempt to rebuild add ongoing headline risk to the memory/chip sector.
