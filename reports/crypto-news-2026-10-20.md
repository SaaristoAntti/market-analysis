1. **Alpenglow Consensus Upgrade Activates Tomorrow — 150ms Finality Target**  
   Solana's *Alpenglow* upgrade is scheduled to go live on mainnet-beta at approximately 14:50 UTC on October 21, 2026, following roughly 18 months of development. The headline target is **~150-millisecond finality**, down from the current ~12.8 seconds — a ~98.8% improvement.  
   Sources: [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2026/10/14/solana-sets-oct-21-for-alpenglow-upgrade-targeting-150-millisecond-finality) · [KuCoin](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/solana-s-alpenglow-mainnet-upgrade-activates-on-october-21) · [Bitcoin.com](https://news.bitcoin.com/crypto-news/solanas-alpenglow-arrives-oct-21-major-speed-boost/) · [crypto.news](https://crypto.news/solana-alpenglow-october-21-150ms-finality/)
