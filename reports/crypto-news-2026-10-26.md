- **24h range:** $99.88 – $102.27

### Top News

1. **Alpenglow Consensus Upgrade Goes Live — Finality Cut to 150ms**
   - Solana activated the Alpenglow upgrade on approximately October 21, 2026, replacing the TowerBFT consensus mechanism with Votor. Transaction finality dropped from ~12.8 seconds to ~150 milliseconds following governance proposal SIMD-0326, which passed with 98.27% validator support. This is the most significant protocol change in Solana's history.
   - Source: [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-activates-alpenglow-upgrade-cuts-transaction-finality-to-150ms/)

2. **Solana ETFs Post $17M+ in Weekly Outflows**
   - Solana spot ETFs recorded over $8 million in outflows on Friday alone, pushing the weekly total past $17 million — contrasting with XRP ETFs, which resumed inflows. Institutional demand for SOL-backed products remains weak.
   - Source: [Invezz](https://invezz.com/news/2026/10/26/solana-holds-above-100-as-etf-outflows-and-falling-oi-weigh-on-recovery/) / [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/xrp-etfs-resume-winning-streak-while-sol-funds-stay-in-the-red)

3. **SOL Posts Third Consecutive Weekly Decline**
   - Solana has dropped for three straight weeks, now clinging to the psychologically important $100 support level. Futures open interest declined to $6.27B from $6.34B the prior week, a sign of reduced speculative activity.
   - Source: [FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/cryptocurrencies/news/solana-price-forecast-sol-stalls-around-100-amid-weak-institutional-demand-202610260656)

4. **Slot Time Cut to 200ms Under SIMD-0525**
   - Separate from Alpenglow, Solana implemented a staged reduction in slot time from 400ms toward 200ms (SIMD-0525), with the first cut to 350ms beginning around epoch 1020 in late August. The combined effect with Alpenglow positions Solana as the fastest major L1 network by finality.
