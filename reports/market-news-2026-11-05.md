### The July 2026 Collapse
- **Peak AUM:** ~$45 billion
- **Post-collapse AUM:** ~$10 billion
- **Estimated losses:** $20B–$35B (estimates vary by source)
- **Cause:** The fund used up to **400% leverage** on concentrated AI infrastructure positions.
